Design system

Professional broadcast tooling: restrained surfaces, one warm accent, and colour reserved for meaning. Every component below is rendered by the same helpers the real screens call, so this page cannot drift from the product: if a chip changes shape here, it changed shape everywhere.

02 · Colour

Colour encodes state, role or provenance. Colour that encodes nothing is bone, ink or grey. Meanings are unchanged from the previous system: gold is published, amber needs a human, cyan is working, emerald is evidence, red is blocked.
