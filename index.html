    What this site does not claim

    A tracker that hides its limits is just a rumour with a stylesheet. These are ours, in plain language.

    Teach it to read a broadcast it has never seen

    Every production puts the hero portraits somewhere slightly different, so each new broadcast needs calibrating once. The wizard does that in your browser: drop in a video or a few screenshots, it finds the ten hero slots, you check them on zoomed crops, and it hands you the layout file.

    Nothing is uploaded and nothing is installed — the detection runs on your own machine, in the tab. A calibration for a broadcast nobody has covered yet is the single most useful thing anyone can contribute, and anyone may send one.
