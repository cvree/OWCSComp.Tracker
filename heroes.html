How to read this board

  • Pick rate is the share of tracked team-map appearances this hero was in. One hero played by one team for a whole map counts once, however many frames it appeared in.
  • Win rate counts only maps with a recorded winner. A blank is “no decided map yet”, never a zero — zero is a claim.
  • Sample is the honest version of a tier letter. Three pips is a rate worth quoting; one amber pip means the number is arithmetic over a handful of maps and should be read as “what happened”, not “what is true”.
  • Swaps are mid-map hero changes confirmed across consecutive samples. Candidates that failed that test are published as rejections on the swap evidence page rather than counted here.
  • Bans are match facts imported from official sources, never inferred from video, and never mixed into pick rates.
  • Heroes not yet sighted have no verified appearance in a tracked match. That is a statement about this tracker's coverage, not about how the hero is played — why coverage is small →
